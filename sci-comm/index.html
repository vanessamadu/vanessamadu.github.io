Wisdom from Wonderland

Video series · All years

Four short talks. One Wonderland. Actual mathematics.

Using Lewis Carroll's Alice in Wonderland as a jumping-off point, each talk takes one idea — data science, statistical inference, ocean science, climate attribution — and makes it genuinely exciting in under twenty minutes. Designed for curious people of any background, each episode can be extended to 30–45 minutes for school or university audiences.

Long-term projects · Programmes · Initiatives

Public engagement & outreach

Hello World Hack

Co-founded and co-designed a coding workshop for girls aged 7–10, awarded £2,000 from the Imperial College Societal Engagement Seed Fund. Initially planned for 120+ children, pivoted to a successful virtual event during COVID-19 and has since become an annual fixture.

60+ children in person · 30 virtually · Now annual

RCSU Science Challenge — Chair

Chaired the annual science communication competition for school students (aged 15–18) in 2022–23. Introduced Science Shorts video series, established a partnership with the Young Scientists Journal, recruited judges including Kathy Sykes (BBC) and Tom Crawford (Numberphile), and secured a £1,000 pledge from Professor Lord Robert Winston.

80% increase in participation · ~180 school-division participants worldwide

Project Insight

Founded a website providing STEM role models for minority students through interviews with professionals across industry and academia. Grew the team to 6 members and built relationships with 30+ STEM professionals, including the Global CEO of Publicis Sapient and the President of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications.

700+ website visitors · 6-person team

Imperial College London Outreach

Academic Leader and Mentor across multiple outreach programmes (2021–2025) including the Sutton Trust Summer School, STEM Futures, STEM Potential, and Insights residential programmes. Created content on fluid dynamics, probability, fractals, number theory, and infinity for Year 10–12 students from underrepresented backgrounds, and co-led Primary Science Club and the Primary Maths Transition Programme.
